### Hi, I'm Wesley 👋

I co-founded [Aragon AI](https://www.aragon.ai/) in late 2022, fine-tuning open-source image models before ChatGPT came out. In three years it became the world's most popular AI headshot company: 2.5M+ users and nearly $10M in annual revenue with a team of around 10. I was CEO until I exited in mid-2026 to work on my next thing.

Before that I was a founding engineer at two venture-backed startups and an engineer at Microsoft, and studied CS and data science at the University of Michigan.
